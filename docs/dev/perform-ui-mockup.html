Perform Mode Screens

Mockup v22 of the Perform screens at the pedal's 128 × 64 pixels, drawn with the pedal's own fonts, knob, fader, switch, tabs, lists and softkeys (ported from the 2.10 firmware). The clock runs at 120 BPM from when the page opens. The moves are the transition example from the brief.

Try this

  1. Footswitch once: its target is FX4 Ftsw, the looper's own footswitch. The top bar says REC IN 3 BEATS and its fill blinks faster as the bar line comes. Footswitch again: PLAY IN …, then it plays. The LOOPER menu's REC END action glides MIX XFade to B100 over 1 bar: watch the fader.
  2. Turn knob 2 (FX3 Mix): its knob icon blinks while the Follow waits, faster as the cycle line comes. Then Mix goes back to 30.
  3. Press Cursor ◄ (FX1 Fdbk, a dub throw): it glides up over 1 bar and drops back 2 bars after the press. Its softkey is inverted while Fdbk is away from its default, and blinks while a move waits.
  4. Press Cursor ▼ (FX2 OnOff): FX2 goes off now and back on at the next bar.
  5. Two quick footswitch presses: aimed at FX4 Ftsw, that is the looper's own delete, and the LOOPER menu's DELETE action sets XFade back to A100. There is no Double gesture.
  6. Perform Setup › LOOPER: SYNC and one row per loop event, each with a target and one action (value, next, lag).
  7. Setup › KNOB 1 › TARGET: first the module, then its parameter. Every effect has OnOff; the looper also has Ftsw.
  8. Clock: stopped. Every quantized move fires at once.
  9. Menu: EFFECT CHAIN, DELETE EFFECT UNIT, PERFORM MODE, SETTINGS, MIDI SETTINGS, VERSION. PERFORM MODE opens a list like MIDI SETTINGS', with icons: ENTER (with the hint "(▲ TO EXIT)") and SETUP. Knob 3 press opens. Cursor ▲ leaves Perform; knob 2 press leaves Setup, back to the menu.
  10. Setup › any control › TARGET › FX4 › Roll: a button press can throw the looper's Roll (OFF, 1/2 … 1/32).

Every move: VALUE (- for unused, then the target's range), NEXT and LAG. A move fires once and waits for the next line of its NEXT grid (bars and cycles count from bar 1). A Follow lands exactly its NEXT after its Action or Release landed (2 BAR = 2 bars later). A Turn's Follow has nothing to count from, so it waits for the next line.

Screens

  • Top bar = the cycle counter, drawn like the looper effect's status box, with the looper state on the left and bar.beat/cycle on the right. A gap in the top and bottom edges marks each bar line, and a dotted line crosses the bar there (inverted under the fill), skipping text ink and the 1 px around it.
  • Knob columns as on a stock knob page: effect name, parameter name, the target's own stock control, the value. The 24-row control leaves room for three labels only in the 5-px softkey font, so all three use it.
  • Blinking, like the loop bar: while a move waits, its whole column (name, control, value) fills in step with the loop bar, faster as the move comes. The column is the same width as the softkey under it, with a clear row on every side, so it never touches the top bar or the softkeys. Its softkey swaps between the filled box and just its arrow and name. Moves with no time to count down blink slowly.
  • Looper targets blink too: a control the looper is about to move (a REC START, REC END, STOP, PLAY or PASS action) blinks like a waiting move, from the moment the loop event's time is known: while armed, while stopping, during a fixed-length take, and before each next pass. Every looper action but DEL waits for the next cycle, so REC, STOP and PLAY targets blink from the press until the cycle starts; only DEL is immediate. The loop bar, the columns, the softkeys and the footswitch LED all use the same countdown blink on the beat clock, so they flip together.
  • Bottom row: stock knob-page softkeys (filled, like EXIT) with the target's effect name, cut to fit right of the arrow (TREMOL). The cursor arrow sits inside each box at its left: above the box there is one row too few once a column blinks.
  • Crossfades read like a pan: A100 (all A) … A50 … AB (centre) … B50 … B100 (all B). Same text on the mixer pages (build M3b8).
  • Setup keys, the same on every list: knob 1 turn scrolls, knob 3 press opens, knob 2 press goes back. Knob 3 turn changes a row that has a value. On a move's knob page the three knobs edit Value, Next and Lag.

Footswitch and LOOPER menu

  • The footswitch is a normal control: one TARGET, and press and hold moves, like a cursor button.
  • Aim it at FX4 Ftsw and it is the looper's footswitch: press rec/play/stop, double press delete. Any control can be aimed there.
  • LOOPER menu (only when the patch has the looper): SYNC OWN/SLAVE, then REC START, REC END, PLAY, STOP, DELETE, LOOP START (each pass). Each has a target and one action.
  • OWN records from the next cycle start until the cycle start after the second press, so a loop is always whole cycles; SLAVE from the next cycle start for one cycle. STOP and PLAY also land on the next cycle start.
