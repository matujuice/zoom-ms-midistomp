Try this

  1. Select DirtB, turn knob 1 left: it crosses [ROUTE] onto Bus A.
  2. Knob 3 while it is on a bus: it jumps to Bus B. Unselect it (knob 1 press) and knob 3 moves only the cursor to the other bus row; knob 1 then walks that row left to right.
  3. Select [MIX] and move it: the effects it passes go to Post or back to the buses.
  4. Open [ROUTE] (knob 3 press), set Mode to Serial: one line, no A/B tags. Back to Paral: every effect returns to its bus.
  5. Hold Cursor ◄ next to EuGate: a new effect appears; Cursor ▲ until HD Hall: it doesn't fit the DSP, so DSP FULL shows and it is bypassed. Delete another effect and it comes back.
  6. PEDAL INPUT: STEREO: the mono effects get a ! tag (Smart Warning). Then [ROUTE] Mode MonoSer: the lines join just before [ROUTE] and the tags after it go away.
  7. Hold knob 2's ● for a moment: DELETE? appears. Press it again to delete the effect, or anything else to cancel.
  8. [ROUTE] Send 0 and [MIX] XFade 100: the ! badge says the output is silent.
  9. PEDAL INPUT: STEREO, then [ROUTE] Mode Split: L goes only to Bus A, R only to Bus B.
  10. Cursor onto OUT (knob 1 to the far left), OPEN: Master Level only (with Output Mode Split: Master L on knob 1, Master R on knob 2). Select [MIX] and turn knob 3 (or OPEN, page 1, knob 2): Output Mode (Stereo, Mono or Split). The lines inside [MIX] and the OUT plugs follow. IN opens Input Level (with [ROUTE] Mode Split: Input L and Input R).
  11. Raise Input Level, a bus level or Master Level above 100: a LIMIT tag shows over the OUT plugs. [MIX] page 3: Mute A / Mute B / Solo; the muted bus is dotted after its last effect.

Taken from the photos

  • 128 × 64 pixels, each pixel about 1.45 times taller than wide.
  • Stock covers, 38 × 23 at x 3 / 45 / 87 (4-pixel gaps): top and bottom edges 3 rows thick with stepped corners, 1-pixel sides, a dot inside each corner, the name on 2 lines of 5 characters in the stock 6 × 7 font (2-pixel vertical strokes). Here 38 × 19, see below. Dotted when OFF.
  • Stock CHO (selected, ADD EFFECT UNIT): the cover inverted inside a box 2 pixels larger, so a 2-pixel square frame whose inner corners follow the cover's, the cover's outline left clear as the gap, the inside filled and the name inverted.
  • Stock EXIT: a square 1-pixel box, the word in the stock font, an arrow icon below it.
  • Title bar, softkey bar. The knob pages copy the stock tab row (name, page tabs 1 2 3), the knob arcs and TEMPO / EXIT / PAGE.

Changed from stock

  • The crane and the lift space are gone. Bus A sits where the lifted cover used to go, and Bus B in the stock row. Pre and Post sit between them, on the line where the fork splits.
  • Cursor: only the outline of the CHO frame (1 px, square, 2 px outside the item, so a 1-px gap). Selected: the stock CHO look, the item inverted inside that box (2-px frame, 1-px gap, filled inside). Same for [ROUTE] and [MIX]; IN and OUT get the cursor outline only.
  • [ROUTE] and [MIX] are stock EXIT boxes (square, 1-px line) as wide as a cover and as tall as both bus rows: the name in the stock font and an icon of the current mode, signal right to left, an arrowhead on each output. [ROUTE] Mode: Serial one line, Paral one line splitting into two, Split two lines, MonoSer two lines joining into one. [MIX] Output Mode: Stereo two lines joining and splitting again, Mono two lines joining into one, Split two lines. The icon follows knob 3 and the knob pages.
  • Two cover rows don't fit at the stock 23 rows: covers are 19 rows (edges 2 rows thick instead of 3, the outer row 1 px shorter at each end; no clear rows around the name) in the stock font, and the CHO frame is 2 px all round, so the bus rows sit 3 rows apart.
  • For the same reason there is no hint row: the ◄ ► scroll arrows sit in the title bar ends, the Smart Warning text replaces the title while the cursor is on that effect, and the knob hints sit at the left end of their softkey ({} turns, HOLD for DELETE, ^v for Bus A ↔ B).
  • IN and OUT are the stock plug icons copied pixel for pixel (body, ridges, ring and rounded tip), one per channel (the upper one is L, as printed on the case). Only the cable is thinner. Mono: one plug, one line. Stereo: two plugs and double lines.
  • The lines split in the gap before [ROUTE] and join in the gap after [MIX]; those two gaps are 7 pixels instead of 4. Where two lines cross without joining, one hops over the other. With no effect between a jack and [ROUTE] or [MIX], each plug's cable runs straight into the node at its own row.
  • Scrolling is stock: 3 columns, the cursor's column always in the middle. A bus column holds its Bus A and Bus B effect one above the other.
  • The knob pages show the bus as a filled tag at the right of the tab row.

Levels, Mute / Solo and LIMIT

  • Every level (Input, Bus A / B in [ROUTE] and [MIX], Master) goes 0-150, 100 = unity. IN and OUT get a second knob (Input R, Master R) only in Split; otherwise knob 1 drives both sides.
  • [ROUTE] page 1: Send, Mode. [MIX] page 1: Crossfade, Curve. Page 2: Bus A Level, Bus B Level, Phase Invert (Bus B). Page 3: Mute A, Mute B, Solo (Off / A / B). Solo overrides Mute.
  • The LIMIT tag here is a simulation: it shows when the product of the gains on the path to OUT is above 1.0 (Serial: Input x Master; Parallel: Input x bus levels x Master, on any bus that is not muted). The real tag follows the safety limiter.

Still open

Drawn here as a ! tag above the cover (in the top-left corner on bus rows, which have no room above), the warning text in the title bar while the cursor is on it, and a ! tab plus the text on its knob pages.
  2. Mono Serial ([ROUTE] Mode MonoSer, stereo input only): Serial with L and R summed to mono at [ROUTE] (the join is drawn just before it, where its splits are drawn too).
  3. DSP FULL: the popup here is a placeholder; the real one is the stock DSP FULL popup from the effect screen. The DSP costs are made up (budget 100).
