# MIDISTOMP MOD v2.0.4

Made alongside **[effects pack v2.0.4](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-zdl-effects-pack/releases/tag/v2.0.4)**, both fixing a crackle reported from a live set: a full 6-effect chain (Washout, DJFilter x2, GRDLOOP, Takeoff, Louder) crackling under PERFORM, worst with DJFilter and Washout swept together.

**Effects pack v2.0.4:** Washout, DJFilter and Takeoff cost less DSP, so a full chain no longer crosses the pedal's DSP budget. DJFilter also stops clicking across DJ 0 and under PERFORM steps.

**Firmware**
- **PERFORM audio load:** the per-block work is much lighter. The screen redraws only the LCD rows that changed, at most once per 32 ms; a lag writes one effect's knobs per poll, round robin; the grid clock runs every 8th audio block while nothing waits or lags, costing the block nothing the rest of the time.
- **Program Change always reloads the saved patch.** Re-selecting the current patch now drops any live tweaks and restores it exactly as saved, instead of quietly saving over the saved copy first (#68, thanks Andrhodes).
- **SysEx edits reach all six effects.** Editors can change knobs and on/off live in effects 4-6 too, not only 1-3 (#72, thanks Roman Rusinov). SysEx `0x31` no longer renames the patch; editors rename by writing the whole patch.
- **Overclock, opt-in: 360 MHz.** Off by default; builds a second updater (see Install) for pedals tested at that clock. Tested on an MS-70CDR and an MS-60B.

**Install:** two updaters this time. Download **MIDISTOMP-builder-Windows.exe** below and drop Zoom's official **MS-70CDR v2.10** Windows updater on it (also for the MS-50G and MS-60B); it writes **MIDISTOMP V2.0 Updater.exe** (stock clock, the safe default) and **MIDISTOMP V2.0 Updater (360MHz OC).exe** (360 MHz). Run whichever one you want flashed. On a Mac, use **MIDISTOMP-builder-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip** or **MIDISTOMP-builder-Mac-Intel.zip**: [On a Mac](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-midistomp/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#on-a-mac). Recovery: Zoom's original updater. The pedal's menu shows **V2.0.4** on MENU > SYSTEM.

No download here contains Zoom's code: the builders make the MIDISTOMP updater from your own copy of Zoom's.

Bugs and ideas: [issues](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-midistomp/issues). Have fun!
