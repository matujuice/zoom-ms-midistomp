# MIDISTOMP MOD v2.0.1

Fixes from pedal tests and your reports, plus a few small features.

**New**
- **Tap tempo by CC (#53):** CC 82 taps the tempo, without opening the tempo screen.
- **IDLE KALEIDO (#54):** KALEIDO as a screen saver, at power-on and after 10 s to 2 min untouched. Set it in SETTINGS.
- **Fast knobs:** Perform, node and tempo knobs take bigger steps when you turn them fast, like the stock effect knobs.

**Perform mode fixes**
- A Perform knob shows the same values and units as the knob it moves (an effect's own steps, not 0..200).
- NEXT is now NOW, 1-4 beats or 1-32 bars, counted from the press. The cycle fractions are gone.
- A Follow move waits until its action has finished, not just the press.
- HOLD set to NOW fires at once, even when the press waits for NEXT.
- Turning a knob no longer cancels its queued press.
- The bar and the LED blink in step. With GRDLOOP in the patch they follow the looper's own bar, exactly as on the looper's page.
- A waiting button blinks its own softkey, not the cycle bar.
- The looper bar shows only on GRDLOOP's own page, not on its knob pages.

**Other fixes**
- **MASTER (#55):** MASTER and KALEIDO pause their analysis while the audio load is very high, so the controls keep working. Patches over 100% CPU still click: keep an eye on the CPU % in the MENU title bar.
- **Silent delay after GRDLOOP (pack #41):** the output limiter now drops broken samples, so a delay or reverb can't go silent until a power cycle.

**Update GRDLOOP too:** Perform's loop events need **GRDLOOP from effects pack v2.0.1**. GRDLOOP now holds up to about 23 s stereo or 43 s mono.

**Your Perform setups:** Perform moves now store the target's own value, so setups saved in v2.0 are cleared. Patches, routing and installed effects are kept. Back up your patches before you update anyway, just in case.

**Install:** same as v2.0. Download **MIDISTOMP-builder-Windows.exe** below and drop Zoom's official **MS-70CDR v2.10** Windows updater on it (also for the MS-50G and MS-60B), then run the `MIDISTOMP V2.0.1 Updater.exe` it writes. The boot screen reads **v2.0.1**. On a Mac, use **MIDISTOMP-builder-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip** or **MIDISTOMP-builder-Mac-Intel.zip**: [On a Mac](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-midistomp/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#on-a-mac). Recovery: Zoom's original updater.

No download here contains Zoom's code: the builders make the MIDISTOMP updater from your own copy of Zoom's.

**Effects:** [zoom-ms-zdl-effects-pack v2.0.1](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-zdl-effects-pack/releases/tag/v2.0.1): GRDLOOP fixes, GRDELAY's reverse no longer clicks, and Takeoff fires at once with new Length and Tail knobs.

Bugs and ideas: [issues](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-midistomp/issues). Have fun!
