# MIDISTOMP MOD v2.0

The big one. Your MultiStomp gets routing, a performance screen, a looper, studio meters and a light show.

**New**
- **EFFECT CHAIN ROUTING:** your chain can branch now. The Effect Chain screen sets the routing: split the chain into two parallel buses (Bus A and Bus B) between Pre and Post effects. The modes are Serial, Parallel, Split (L to A, R to B) and Mono Serial. The `[ROUTE]` and `[MIX]` nodes give you send and crossfade (Smooth, DJ or Cut curve), bus levels, phase invert, mute and solo, and a Stereo, Mono or Split output. The nodes don't use effect slots: you still get 6 effects. A safety limiter at the very end keeps the output from clipping.
- **PERFORM MODE:** one screen where every knob, cursor key (◄ ▼ ►) and the footswitch has a target: an effect knob, an effect's on/off, a routing control or the looper. Every gesture (turn, press, hold) can wait for the next beat, bar or cycle, glide over time (Lag), and schedule a Follow move. Waiting moves blink on screen and on the LED. Saved per patch.
- **LOOPER:** support for GRDLOOP from the effects pack, a tempo-synced looper that records whole bars. Up to about 20 s stereo or 39.5 s mono, latched rolls, and the footswitch records, stops, plays and deletes on the grid. It works seamlessly with Perform mode, and its state shows on the LED.
- **MASTER:** six metering pages for the pedal's output: SPECTRUM, SCOPE with tuner, STEREO, LOUDNESS (LUFS), IN METER and OUT METER (with the limiter's gain reduction). It costs nothing while closed.
- **KALEIDO:** a full-screen visual that dances to your sound and flashes on every beat of the MIDI clock.
- **MIDI:** CC 85-101 for every routing control. The looper's on/off CC is its footswitch.
- **DSP 300:** the DSP limit goes from 225 to 300, so heavier patches fit. The MENU title bar shows the patch's DSP out of 300 and the CPU %. Keep an eye on the CPU: a patch near the top may click.
- **SYSTEM** (was VERSION): the version, the CPU load, the load of each effect, and the share of each part of MIDISTOMP (MODS).

**QOL improvements**
- EXIT goes back to the MENU entry you left.
- SETTINGS > INPUT MONO/STEREO (set STEREO on an MS-70CDR for the Split modes). MIDI is now the last row of SETTINGS.
- Input and Master levels go up to 150 (100 = unity).
- Everything from v1.2 stays: Tempo Lock, HOLD FOR, button combos, faster scrolling, plus MIDI CC/PC, CC 80/81 patch up/down, MIDI clock and Start/Stop.

**Your patches:** patches and installed effects are kept. Patches saved before 2.0 load in Serial mode with every effect in one line, so they sound as before. Patches made in 2.0 play their effects in one line on older firmware.

**Install:** download MIDISTOMP-builder.exe below and drop Zoom's official **MS-70CDR v2.10** Windows updater on it (use it for the MS-50G and MS-60B too). Then run the `MIDISTOMP V2.0 Updater.exe` it writes. The boot screen reads **v2.0**. Recovery: Zoom's original updater.

**Mac:** download **MIDISTOMP V2.0 Updater** (Mac zip) below, or build it from Zoom's Mac updater with `scripts/midistomp_builder.py`. A much easier Mac install is on the way.

**Effects:** GRDLOOP and the other MIDISTOMP effects live in [zoom-ms-zdl-effects-pack](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-zdl-effects-pack).

Full steps and every new screen: [user guide](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-midistomp/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md). Bugs and ideas: [issues](https://github.com/matujuice/zoom-ms-midistomp/issues). Have fun!
