Master Meter Screens

Mockup v8 of MENU › MASTER at the pedal's 128 × 64 pixels: five pages that watch the pedal's final output (after the output limiter). Stock fonts, title bar, frames, lists and softkeys (ported from the 2.10 firmware); the live graphics take after themanro's spectrum and scope effects. The sound is a made-up drum, bass and pad loop at 120 BPM, generated in the page, with a louder chorus every 8 bars.

Try this

  1. Knob 3 press: next page (Spectrum › Scope › Stereo › Loudness › Out meter › In meter › Spectrum). The right softkey names the next page.
  2. Knob 1 press: the page's option, named in the left softkey: FALL on SPECTRUM, SPAN on SCOPE, ZOOM on STEREO, RESET on LOUDNESS and both meter pages. Knob 1 turn steps the option up or down and stops at the ends.
  3. Knob 2 press: EXIT to MENU. The audio copy stops there. MASTER in the list opens it again on the page you left (knob 1 or knob 3 press).
  4. Hot input pushes the mix 9 dB into the output limiter: the LIMIT bar moves, DYNAMICS shrinks, and on IN METER the input clips: CLIP on L and R, INPUT CLIP below, CLIPS counts.
  5. Wait for the chorus (every 8 bars): 3 SEC steps up, the graph on LOUDNESS climbs, RANGE grows.
  6. Switching patterns in one patch: press knob 1 (RESET) on LOUDNESS or a meter page. AVERAGE, RANGE, PEAK, LIMIT, CLIPS and the graph start again, on all three pages.
  7. Change patch does the same reset by itself, as switching patches will on the pedal.

SCOPEThe mono sum, min/max per column, dotted centre line. Each redraw starts on a rising zero crossing, so a steady note stands still: on a low-passed copy (about 700 Hz, so hats cannot fire it), the trigger arms when the signal goes below −25% of its recent peak and fires on the next sample at or above 0. Top right: the time across the screen and the frequency, then the nearest note and how far off it is in cents (A4 = 440 Hz, C4 = middle C). The bar on the right is a tuner: it fills up when sharp, down when flat, ±50 cents at the ends, nothing when in tune. The crossings are found between samples, so it stays exact on high notes too.SPAN: AUTO / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 24 ms. CPU: almost nothing in the audio path

  • Audio side, only while MASTER is open: copy the block's 16 output floats (8 L, 8 R, after the limiter) and the limiter's gain into a 4096-frame ring (32 KB), then bump a write counter. About 30 cycles per block.
  • MASTER closed: one flag test per block, nothing else.
  • Everything else runs on the UI side, in idle time: K-weighting filters and gating for LUFS, the FFT, the scope trigger and its period search (one pass over about 3,100 samples per redraw), goniometer, correlation, peaks and RMS. Only the open page draws; loudness keeps counting on every page so I and LRA don't stop while you flip pages.
  • The FFT (1024 points, Hann) runs only on SPECTRUM, at 10 to 15 frames a second.
  • The ring holds 93 ms. The UI must read it at least 11 times a second; if it falls behind, the write counter shows it and the missed part is skipped (loudness just has a gap).
  • I and LRA use a fixed 0.1 LU histogram (about 2 KB), not a list of blocks, so memory never grows.

Memory

The ring and the code take about 48 KB from the start of looper region 1 (0xC010A000). Region 1 moves up, which costs about 1% of loop time. The C section has only 416 bytes left, so MASTER gets its own section.

Limits of v1

  • Sample peak, not true peak. True peak needs 4× oversampling on the UI side; maybe in v2.
  • 1-bit screen: no colours, so RMS is solid and peak is dotted.
  • Loudness counts only while MASTER is open. Leaving pauses it; coming back carries on.
