MIDISTOMP Patches

Local patch manager for MS-70CDR / MS-50G. Reads all 50 patches, shows effects and MIDISTOMP routing, renames, edits routing and MIXER settings, backs up and restores. Close Zoom Effect Manager and other editors first.
Not connected.
MIXER settings (Mode, levels, crossfades) and PERFORM live outside the patch, matched by its effects and name. On MIDISTOMP firmware the editor keeps the MIXER and PERFORM settings when you rename a patch or move its effects; on older firmware they go back to defaults. Backups carry MIXER settings, not PERFORM. Routing (bus, Input R, Master R, Output, mutes, solo) is inside the patch and is always kept.
